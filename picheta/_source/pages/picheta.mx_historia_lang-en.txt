FINE DINING MERIDA
PICHETA, THE RESTAURANT IN THE HEART OF CENTRO

In a building more than 350 years old, in the heart of the center of Mérida, we find Picheta fine dining restaurant, focused on generating a full sensorial experience, dedicated to establish itself as a union drum between the pre-Hispanic, the colonial and the current. A place of gastronomy of origin, evolution and authors. Picheta offers food with a heart, an experience of experiences: vibrant, passionate, warm, exciting and deeply Yucatecan.

A discreet entrance reveals the best way to live an immersive gastronomic experience, a journey of contemporary tradition, a hug to the soul; all developed by a group of chefs passionately dedicated to taking our gastronomy to unpublished places.

Get excited with us, live in first person the passage to a world where food is lived from another point of view, with attention and absolute, multisensory pleasure. A space where we celebrate our origin by allowing it to evolve with us; where more than follow recipes, we communicate emotion.
