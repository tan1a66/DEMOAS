EL CORAZÓN DE LA GASTRONOMÍA YUCATECA

In the heart of downtown Mérida, in a building more than 350 years old, a discrete entrance reveals our proposal of Contemporary Yucatecan Cuisine, with resident chefs and guest authors, that develop unique dishes from locally sourced ingredients, preserving and enriching our gastronomy through a sensorial experience.
